## 2. How I Checked My Work

I reviewed and verified the results at different stages to make sure the work was correct and consistent.

- **Row Count Verification:** Confirmed that the original 5,000 rows became 4,975 rows after removing 25 duplicate records.
- **KPI Verification:** Checked the KPIs using Excel formulas, SQL queries and Power BI. All three methods produced the same results:
  - Total complaints: 4,975
  - Open complaints: 310
  - Closed complaints: 4,665
  - On-time response percentage: 90.1%
  - Average response time: 8.8 days
- **SQL Verification:** Executed all 12 SQL queries and checked their outputs. The results are saved in `sql_outputs.txt`.
- **Open Complaints:** Excluded open complaints from the on-time percentage and average response time calculations, as required by the project brief.
- **Timely Flag Verification:** Checked that the Yes/No timely response flag matched the response dates and due dates.
- **Power BI Verification:** Tested the dashboard slicers and checked that the monthly chart was arranged in the correct month order.
- **Findings Verification:** Reviewed the report findings and ensured they included the effect of company size on complaint counts, the difference between a timely response and customer satisfaction, and the fact that October 2026 represents a partial month.
